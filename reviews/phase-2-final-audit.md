# Phase 2 Final Audit — Master Role Universe v1.0 Candidate

Status: REVIEW RECOMMENDATION — NOT CANONICAL

Audit target: `roles/master-role-universe.md` (55-role candidate)

## Audit objective

Test the candidate against four architecture goals:
1. broad applicability across general businesses;
2. EU-funded programme development and implementation;
3. €200m+ investment / infrastructure project preparation;
4. digital products, software, databases, data and AI systems.

The audit also checks for hidden duplication, missing first-class methodologies and capability-map drift.

## Result

The 55-role candidate is structurally sound. No major redesign is required. Four material capability gaps remain before Phase 2 should be approved.

## Gap 1 — Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL)

### Recommendation
Add first-class role: **Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning Specialist**.

### Why first-class
MEL has a distinct methodology and recurring professional artifacts: theory of change, intervention logic, logical framework, indicator architecture, baseline/target methodology, monitoring plan, evaluation questions, results framework, impact evidence and lessons-learned loops.

It is not equivalent to Deliverables / Reporting. Reporting describes implementation; MEL assesses whether interventions produce intended results and how those results are evidenced.

### Stress-test relevance
Required or strongly useful for EU programmes, development programmes, institutional projects, social dialogue, skills/VET programmes and impact-oriented public funding.

## Gap 2 — FP&A / Management Finance

### Recommendation
Add first-class role: **FP&A / Management Finance Specialist**.

### Why first-class
The current finance roles are primarily project/investment/transaction oriented. A general operating business needs recurring management-finance methodology: budgeting, rolling forecasts, cash planning, management P&L, variance analysis, unit economics, scenario planning, KPI packs and management reporting.

This is distinct from project financial modelling and from Accounting / Financial Due Diligence.

### Stress-test relevance
Required for e-commerce, SaaS, services, manufacturing, hospitality and other ongoing businesses.

## Gap 3 — Data / Business Analytics

### Recommendation
Add first-class role: **Data / Business Analytics Specialist**.

### Why first-class
Data engineering creates and maintains data systems; Research / Market Intelligence analyses markets and external evidence. Neither owns recurring operational analysis of business or programme data: KPI definition, analytical datasets, dashboards, cohort/funnel analysis, performance analysis, descriptive/diagnostic analytics and decision-support reporting.

### Stress-test relevance
Required across digital products, general business operations, institutional dashboards and programme monitoring.

## Gap 4 — Supply Chain / Procurement Operations

### Recommendation
Add first-class role: **Supply Chain & Procurement Operations Specialist**.

### Why first-class
`Procurement / State Aid Specialist` is a legal/public-procurement/compliance profile. It should not absorb commercial sourcing, supplier strategy, inventory, logistics, fulfilment, demand/supply planning and operational procurement.

The capability map explicitly includes Supply Chain under Operations, but the current role universe has no role with this methodology.

### Stress-test relevance
Required for manufacturing, retail, e-commerce, distribution, physical-product businesses and many infrastructure operating models.

## Hidden duplication review

No merge is recommended at this stage for:
- Funding & Bankability Architect vs Project Finance / Transaction Specialist — distinct stage and output logic;
- EU Grants & Programmes Specialist vs EU Programme Implementation & Grant Management Specialist — acquisition vs implementation lifecycle;
- Knowledge & Evidence Steward vs Data Room & Disclosure Manager — epistemic governance vs confidential disclosure governance;
- Product Manager / Business Analyst vs Strategy & Business Analyst — product decision-making vs enterprise/strategic analysis;
- Legal & Regulatory Lead vs Procurement / State Aid Specialist — broad legal ownership vs specialised procurement/state-aid methodology.

## Roles that should remain specialisations, not first-class roles

- Bid / Proposal Management
- Labour Market & Skills Intelligence
- Change Management / Adoption
- Technical Writing / Documentation
- Version Control / Document Configuration
- specific EU programmes
- specific IFIs
- named technologies
- named sectors
- individual finance metrics

These do not currently justify separate role identities across the whole AI-OS.

## Proposed count after audit

Current candidate: 55 professional roles.

Recommended additions:
1. Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning Specialist
2. FP&A / Management Finance Specialist
3. Data / Business Analytics Specialist
4. Supply Chain & Procurement Operations Specialist

Proposed **Master Role Universe v1.0 = 59 professional roles**.

System Control Profiles, Review Profiles, Decision Rights and Skill / Specialisation profiles remain outside this count.

## Stress-test conclusion

### General business
PASS after adding FP&A, Data/Business Analytics and Supply Chain/Procurement Operations.

### EU-funded programmes (e.g. Erasmus+/CoVE, social dialogue, climate adaptation)
PASS after adding MEL.

### €200m+ investment / infrastructure preparation
PASS with current project-development, technical, commercial, finance, IFI, legal, tax, ESG, risk, insurance and data-room roles. Sector expertise remains skill-pack driven / on-demand through Sector Technical Expert.

### Websites / SaaS / portals / admin systems
PASS with Product, UX/IA, Solution Architecture, Full-Stack, API, DevOps, Data/DB, AI/Knowledge Systems, Security and QA roles.

### Databases / analytics / AI systems
PASS after adding Data / Business Analytics Specialist; architecture, engineering and AI-system responsibilities are otherwise adequately separated.

## Phase 2 recommendation

Approve Phase 2 only after human confirmation of the four proposed additions. No further role proliferation is recommended before Phase 3 Role Registry design.
