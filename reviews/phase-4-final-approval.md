## Standing Architecture Rules Preserved

- `ROLE != SKILL != SPECIALISATION != SKILL PACK != MODEL`.
- Role Cards remain authoritative for professional scope, methodology ownership, artifact ownership and authority boundaries.
- Mapping records remain the sole authoritative source for relationship type, trigger and alternative choice condition.
- Skill / Specialisation / Pack activation grants capability applicability only, never professional or human authority.
- Review independence remains outside the Skill Registry.
- Human decisions remain human-controlled.
- AI suggestions do not become canonical automatically.
