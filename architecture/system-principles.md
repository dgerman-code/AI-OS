# AI-OS System Principles

Status: PROPOSED TARGET ARCHITECTURE v1

## Constitutional principles

1. **Role != Model** — professional roles exist independently of any AI provider.
2. **Role != Agent Instance** — a role is activated only when a workflow requires it.
3. **Skills are reusable** — skills and domain packs can be reused across roles and workflows.
4. **Workflow selects roles** — users should not manually choose a large set of agents.
5. **Least Context Principle** — each role receives only the context necessary for its task.
6. **Project and organisational isolation** — context must not leak across organisations, programmes, projects, products, workstreams or tasks.
7. **Evidence before assertion** — material claims should be traceable to sources, evidence, calculations or explicitly marked assumptions.
8. **AI is not canonical authority** — AI suggestions, drafts and analyses do not automatically become approved or canonical truth.
9. **Author != Critical Reviewer** — material work requires independent assurance.
10. **Human authority remains explicit** — designated legal, financial, strategic, methodological, production and publication decisions require human approval.
11. **Models are replaceable runtimes** — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Codex and future models can assume roles without redesigning the Role Registry.
12. **Complexity must be justified** — new roles are created only when a distinct methodology, authority boundary, review requirement or recurring professional artifact justifies them.
13. **Current portfolio does not define system capability** — present projects are stress-test cases, not architectural limits.
14. **Digital Product != Software != Data** — product intent, software behaviour and data architecture are separate competency areas.
15. **Delivery, Assurance and Authority are separate** — who produces, who reviews and who approves must be distinguishable.
16. **Structured handoffs replace uncontrolled agent conversations** — inter-role work is transferred through explicit outputs, evidence, assumptions, risks, open questions and next actions.
17. **Canonical methodology lives outside the model** — methodology, policies and governance must be versioned and governed independently of LLM outputs.
18. **Organisation != Programme != Project != Product != Task** — the operating context hierarchy must preserve these distinctions.

## System boundary

AI-OS is not itself an ERP, CRM, accounting system, audit firm, law firm, investment committee, procurement authority or statutory decision-maker. It may coordinate work with such systems and professionals, but it must preserve the applicable authority boundary.
